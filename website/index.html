The Patchworks standard

More than a contractor. A clearer way to build.

Construction often becomes difficult at the handoffs: one team finishes, another waits, materials arrive late and nobody owns the full picture. Patchworks is designed to bring the work into one coordinated view.

  • Defined scope and responsibilities before work begins
  • Trade-wise assignment and site scheduling
  • Visible progress through photos and updates
  • Milestone verification and documented handover
